Categorization

Categorization & summaries

Books are only as useful as they are readable. Every transaction is categorized to your chart of accounts, vendor names are normalized — the six spellings of the same supplier become one — and the week's changes are summarized: what moved, what repeated, what is new.

Request a sample report See the plans

from Review plan · $995 / mo

What is included

  • Every transaction categorized to your chart of accounts, with the rule that assigned it shown on request
  • Vendor normalization and roll-ups: one name per supplier, one line per category, totals that tie
  • The weekly change summary: spend by category against the trailing four weeks, in sentences as well as numbers
  • Month-to-date and quarter-to-date running views, so the month does not arrive as a surprise
  • Clean handoff to your bookkeeper or CPA — a categorized, explained ledger, not a shoebox
  • Export in your format: QuickBooks, Xero or CSV

What you receive

  • The categorized weekly feed, delivered with the report
  • The change summary, written not charted
  • Monthly recap for the close
  • Year-end package for your CPA

Where it fits

From the Review plan up: this is the layer that turns the four watch sections from a list of flags into an explained, categorized read. Watch clients can add it to a Watch plan.

Questions

Do you replace our bookkeeper or CPA?

No. We sit between the bank feeds and the books: categorized, explained and flagged weekly, so the monthly close is faster and the questions fewer. Your bookkeeper keeps the ledger of record; your CPA files the year.

What systems do you read?

The bank and card feeds you connect, read-only, plus QuickBooks or Xero if you run them. Exports and CSVs work too — some clients prefer directed exports to connected feeds, and both are supported.
