Plans, setup and estimator

How it starts

From the first call to the Tuesday cadence in about two weeks. The baseline report usually recovers the setup fee before the second month.

  1. 1

    The call

    Thirty minutes on your entities, your accounts, your systems and who does the books today. If the fit is not there we say so and tell you what we would do instead.

  2. 2

    Connect & baseline

    You connect the bank and card feeds read-only — or we take directed exports if you prefer — and we rebuild the last 12 to 24 months into the recurring register, the vendor list, the document checklist and your flag rules. One-time fee, about ten business days.

  3. 3

    The baseline report

    The first report is about the history, not the week: the creep that accumulated, the duplicates already paid, the documents that never arrived. Most clients recover the setup fee here or in the first quarter.

  4. 4

    The Tuesday cadence

    Monday we read the week. Tuesday morning the report arrives: cash trends, recurring changes, duplicate candidates, missing documents, anomalies explained. You act on what matters and ignore the rest.

Questions we get every week

What exactly do I get each week?

A written report on Tuesday morning: cash position and trend, what changed in recurring expenses, duplicate-invoice candidates with evidence, missing documents, and the anomalies — each explained in a sentence or two. Anything urgent is escalated the same day rather than waiting for Tuesday.

Do you replace our bookkeeper or CPA?

No. We sit between the bank feeds and the books: categorized, explained and flagged weekly so the monthly close is faster and the questions fewer. Your bookkeeper keeps the ledger; your CPA files the year.

How do you connect to our accounts?

Read-only bank and card connections that you own, or directed exports if you prefer. We can see the money; we cannot move it. Connections are revocable in one click, and the details are on the trust page.

What does setup involve?

A one-time connect-and-baseline: we rebuild 12 to 24 months of history into the recurring register, the vendor list and the document checklist, and agree your flag rules in writing. The first report usually pays for it — the history already contains the duplicates and the creep.

What does it cost?

Three monthly plans — Watch, Review and Manage — with prices published on the pricing page, plus the one-time setup. No percentage of savings; the numbers stay honest that way.

How long until the first report?

About ten business days after the feeds connect. The baseline takes the time; the weekly cadence starts immediately after it.
