What is never on the invoice

Percentage of savings

We report every recovered dollar so you can judge the result. Taking a share of it would make the measurement dishonest, so we never do.

Per-flag fees

A quiet week is a good week. You pay the same in February with three flags as in August with thirty.

Software resale

The models, the feeds and the storage are ours to provide. There is no platform license to keep paying for if you leave.

Surprise hours

Anything outside the plan is quoted first, in writing, at the rates above. If we misjudged the scope, that is on us.
