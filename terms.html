Terms of Service

Effective 2023-11-14 · Last updated 2026-03-24

These terms are the agreement between New Beginnings 410 (“AIFolder”, “we”) and the business buying services (“you”). An order form signed by both sides is part of the agreement and takes precedence where it differs. By signing an order or accepting services you accept these terms.

1. Services and orders

We provide the monitoring services described on this site and in your order: the weekly report (cash trends, recurring expense changes, duplicate invoice candidates, missing documents), anomaly explanations, transaction categorization and change summaries, the connect-and-baseline setup, and investigation work. Plans are defined by the feeds and entities listed on the order. Work outside an order is quoted in advance at the published hourly rates and approved in writing before it is performed.

2. Term and renewal

Plans run for twelve months from the order date and continue month to month thereafter; either side may end the month-to-month period on 60 days' written notice. Prices do not change on renewal without 60 days' notice. The setup engagement ends on delivery of the baseline report.

3. Fees and payment

Plans are billed monthly in advance, or annually in advance at the discounted rate, by ACH or card. The connect-and-baseline setup is invoiced at kickoff, net 15. Hourly work is invoiced monthly, net 15. Balances more than 15 days past due accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum the law allows, whichever is less, and we may pause the weekly report's delivery after ten days' written notice. Fees exclude taxes, which are added where they apply. There are no per-flag fees and no percentage-of-savings charges.

4. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you own or are authorized to connect every account, feed and data set in scope, and that you have obtained any consents your employees, customers or regulators require. You will keep report contacts current and tell us promptly about new accounts, entities or systems that should be watched. You remain responsible for the decisions you make with the report's content and for any action taken on a flag — we recommend, you decide.

5. Access and automated actions

Bank and card connections are read-only. We never initiate, approve, move or block payments on your behalf, and nothing in the monitoring changes anything in your accounts. Where we prepare recovery packets, cancellations or chase lists, they are drafts until you approve sending them.

6. Flag rules

The thresholds, sensitive categories, vendors to ignore and escalation contacts agreed at setup are recorded in writing. We follow them, and propose changes as we learn your patterns; changes take effect on your written approval, within two business days.

7. Service levels

Response times per plan are published on the pricing page. The weekly report is delivered each Tuesday except published holidays, when it lands Wednesday. If we miss a scheduled report delivery twice in a calendar quarter, you receive a credit of 10% of that month's plan fee, applied to the next invoice, as your sole remedy for the miss.

8. No guarantee of findings

Monitoring reduces the chance that a duplicate payment, a price increase or a missing document goes unnoticed; it does not eliminate it, and a quiet week is not a warranty that none exist. We do not guarantee that every flag will be material or that every duplicate candidate will be confirmed. Reported figures describe the accounts and periods actually reviewed. We are not your accountant or auditor, and the report is not an audit, a review or a compilation under professional accounting standards.

9. Confidentiality and data

Each side keeps the other's confidential information confidential and uses it only for the agreement, for five years after the agreement ends and indefinitely for trade secrets. We process client data as described on the trust page and in our privacy policy, and will sign a data processing agreement where your regulation requires one. We do not use your data to train models for anyone else. We may use aggregated, de-identified engagement data to improve our methods.

10. Intellectual property

Reports, registers, exports, recovery packets and other deliverables are yours once paid for. Our methods, rules, internal tooling and know-how remain ours, and we may reuse them for other clients provided your confidential information is not disclosed. Third-party and open-source components carry their own licenses, listed on request.

11. Liability

Each side's total liability under the agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the twelve months before the claim, except for breach of confidentiality, a side's indemnity obligations, gross negligence or willful misconduct, and your obligation to pay fees. Neither side is liable to the other for indirect, consequential, special or lost-profit damages, including for amounts that a flag failed to catch or a recovery failed to return. We carry professional liability and cyber insurance and will provide certificates on request.

12. Termination

Either side may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice. You may end the month-to-month period after the initial term on 60 days' notice. On termination we return or delete your data as the trust page describes, hand over the recurring register, vendor list and flag rules, provide reasonable transition assistance at the hourly rates, and invoice work performed.

13. General

Maryland law governs this agreement; courts in Baltimore City, Maryland have jurisdiction. Neither side may assign the agreement without consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale. Notices go to the email on the order and to sales@aifolder.xyz. If a term is unenforceable, the rest stands. We may update these terms with 30 days' notice on this page and by email to client contacts; orders placed after the date are under the new terms.
