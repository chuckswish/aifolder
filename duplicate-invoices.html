Duplicates

Duplicate invoice detection

Double payments hide in fast-moving accounts payable: the same amount on different dates, the same invoice number with a typo, the ACH that followed the card by a week. Every week we compare what was paid against what was invoiced, across accounts, and bring back the candidates — with the evidence attached.

from Watch plan · $495 / mo

What is included

  • Fuzzy matching across vendor, amount, invoice number, date and description — catching $2,410 paid twice, and $2,410 against $2,140
  • Same-week and cross-month checks, so the December duplicate of a November invoice still surfaces
  • Card-versus-bank cross-posting: the autopay that moved from one account to the other and now runs on both
  • Subscription double-billing after a price change or a card expiry, where vendors often re-bill
  • Every candidate ranked by confidence with its evidence: both transactions, the invoice, the PO, the prior history
  • Recovery packet for confirmed duplicates: who to contact, what to say, the reference numbers, the follow-up dates

What you receive

  • The weekly duplicate candidates, ranked and evidenced
  • Recovery packets ready to send on your approval
  • Recovery status tracking until the credit or refund lands
  • A monthly recovered total, in dollars

Where it fits

In every plan, starting with Watch: this is one of the four sections of the weekly report, delivered with its evidence every Tuesday.

Questions

What if you flag something that is not a duplicate?

It happens, and it should: every flag carries its reasoning, and when you mark one as fine we learn the pattern. A false flag that takes ten seconds to dismiss is the price of the real one that pays for the year.

Do you contact the vendors?

On the Review and Manage plans we prepare the packet and, on your approval, send it. On every plan the packet is ready for whoever makes the call.
