A company whose whole job is watching the money.

AIFolder is the financial-operations monitoring practice of New Beginnings 410, based in Baltimore. We watch the money side of small and mid-sized businesses for a living, and send a written report every Tuesday.

The company started in 2023 doing weekly cash reviews for a handful of Baltimore businesses — by hand, in spreadsheets, one long morning at a time. The work was always the same: read every transaction, catch the duplicate and the price step and the missing document, and explain what mattered in language an owner reads with coffee. The tools have changed since; the report has not.

The 410 in the name is Baltimore's area code. It is a reminder of who the first clients were and where most of them still are: companies close enough to drive to, big enough that the money is worth watching, and too small for a full-time controller who would rather not be alone with the question anyway.

We are deliberately not an accounting firm. We do not file, we do not audit, we do not move money — we read, explain and flag, weekly, and we hand your bookkeeper a ledger that is faster to close. Models do the reading; a person writes the report. That division of labor is the product.

Where we work

Clients across Maryland and the Mid-Atlantic — Baltimore, Towson, Columbia, Annapolis and out past the Beltway — and remote engagements anywhere in the United States. On-site in the Baltimore area at no travel charge; elsewhere at cost.

On the team

Analysts from banking operations and forensic accounting, monitoring engineers from data work, and everyone background-checked before they touch a client account.

How we work

Weekly, not eventually

A report that arrives every Tuesday beats a dashboard nobody opens. Cadence is the product.

Explained, not flagged

We write the sentence you would have written if you had the morning free: what it is, why it surfaced, what to do about it.

Read-only by design

Watching the money and moving it are different jobs. We do the first one well enough that you never consider giving us the second.

Alongside your bookkeeper

We are not the replacement for your books or your CPA. We are the layer that makes both faster and their questions fewer.

Who you will talk to

Your named analyst

Reads the week, writes the report, answers the flags. Learns your vendors until the explanations are boring, which is the point.

Monitoring engineer

Connects the feeds, tunes the rules and categories, keeps the ledger readable and the register current.

Founder review

Every Manage client's quarterly review, and any flag above your threshold, gets a second pair of eyes before it goes out.

Your bookkeeper and CPA

Not ours — yours. We hand them a categorized, explained ledger; they close the month and file the year.
